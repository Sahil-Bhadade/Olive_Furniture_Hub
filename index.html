0+ Years crafting furniture

Rooted in Nanded. Built by hand, piece by piece.

Olive Furniture Hub started as a small carpentry workshop in Nanded with one belief — furniture should fit your home, not the other way round. Today we design and manufacture every sofa, bed, table and cabinet in-house, so nothing leaves our workshop until it's exactly right.

We work directly with homeowners, interior designers and businesses across Nanded, turning rough sketches and Pinterest boards into furniture you can actually sit on.

  • Custom design

    Every dimension, finish and fabric chosen by you.

  • Quality materials

    Kiln-dried solid wood, sourced and inspected in-house.

  • Skilled craftsmen

    A local team with decades of combined woodworking experience.

  • Timely delivery

    Clear timelines from order to doorstep, every time.
